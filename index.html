Three ways to work together

Most clients start with the audit. Some stop there, others bring me in to build it with them.

Support audit

Two weeks
A clear picture of where your support stands, and what to fix first. I go through your tickets, tools, workflows, escalations and team, and hand you a prioritized 90-day plan.

Build and scale

Four to eight weeks
Set up support from scratch, or rebuild the one that outgrew itself:
  • Zendesk setup, Jira integration and automations
  • Coverage across time zones with real handoffs
  • KPIs that reward quality, not just speed
  • A knowledge base and AI-assisted first response
  • Hiring profiles and onboarding for new agents

Fractional head of support

Monthly, a few hours a week
Senior support leadership before you're ready for a full-time hire. I own the strategy and goals, coach your team lead, run the feedback loop with product, and help you hire your first full-time head of support.

What I focus on

Coverage across time zones

Shifts with real overlap between regions, so customers don't wait because of where they are.

KPIs that reward quality

Resolution time and workload, balanced with reopen rate and what each person adds to the knowledge base.

A working loop with engineering

Clear escalation paths and linked tickets and bugs, so issues get fixed and customers hear back.

AI with a person behind it

AI for first response, internal knowledge and repetitive work, with a person always owning the outcome.

Your tickets already know what to fix

Support holds the best product feedback in the company, and almost nobody reads it as a whole.

I built an AI pipeline that pulls tickets from Zendesk every two weeks, groups them by root cause, and gives product and engineering a short list of recurring issues worth fixing.

Support stops being the team that closes tickets, and becomes a source for the roadmap. I can set up the same for you.
